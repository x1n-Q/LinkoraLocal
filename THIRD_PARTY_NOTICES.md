# Third-party notices

## cloudflared

Official release packages may bundle the Cloudflare `cloudflared` connector.
`cloudflared` is a separate work maintained by Cloudflare. Its source, license,
and notices are available from the official Cloudflare repository:

https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflared

The Linkora Local repository does not contain a `cloudflared` binary. Release
builders are responsible for obtaining a pinned official build and preserving
the notices required by its license.
